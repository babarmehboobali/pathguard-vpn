# Privacy Policy – PathGuard VPN

**Effective date:** [PLACEHOLDER – update before release]

## Data PathGuard does not collect
We do not collect, log, or sell browsing history, DNS queries, traffic contents, or IP logs of your VPN sessions.

## Third-party servers
- **VPN Gate**: Volunteer servers operated under the University of Tsukuba project. Those operators may log connection metadata according to their own policies.
- **Tor network**: Exit nodes and relays have their own policies.
- **Cloudflare WARP** (optional Fast mode): Nearest location, no country choice. Not affiliated with Cloudflare.

Always use HTTPS for sensitive data.

## Ads
Google AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device information, and approximate location for advertising purposes. Consent is handled via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for EEA/UK users.

## Crash data
None in version 1.0. No crash SDK is included.

## Children
This app is not directed at children under 13.
